02 / THE ARTIST

Angela
Di Tomaso.

Angela Di Tomaso, known as AiDiTi, is an Italian-born multimedia artist whose practice moves between intricate hand-drawn imagery and digital experimentation.

She studied at the Academy of Art in Frosinone and the Brera Academy in Milan before establishing herself in London. Sacred geometry, symbols, nature and human experience run through her drawings, animated narratives, projection mappings and immersive installations.

Her work has appeared at LPM, Kernel, FILE, BYOB and Signal, with installations and exhibitions across Europe, the United States and South America.
